# A Better Web

**We are not here to save you.**

Web3 is a casino. Money rules. Freedom is an afterthought. Scams and DINO projects everywhere. We know this. We've watched it happen.

**We believe in privacy.**

Not as a feature. As a default. Encryption isn't optional—it's how open societies work.

**We believe in sovereignty.**

You own your agency. Wallet, device, person, service. The power to walk away. The power to fork. The power to choose.

**We don't build on their sand.**

AWS. Cloudflare. ICANN. Any can flick a switch. We've watched Big Tech become the gatekeepers. We won't build castles on their infrastructure.

**We are Cypherpunks.**

We write code. We publish it. We build systems that can't be shut down. This is who we are.

**Web3 is for users, not builders.**

Not your product. Not your attention. Not your data. You are the point. We optimize for you.

**We reject capture.**

VC rent-seeking. DINO projects. EEE tactics. New wrappers on old extraction. We build tools, not toll booths.

**Access is a right.**

Most decentralized network means nothing if no one can access it. The last mile is the hardest mile. We build for real users.

**Free speech is non-negotiable.**

Open discourse. No platform judges. We've seen what happens when they do.

**Bitcoin doesn't care.**

It just works. Don't worship money. Use it.

**IP enforcement is ending.**

Open P2P makes it impossible. Patronage replaces licensing. We build for reality.

**We are not a product.**

Public good. CC0. Fork us. Challenge us. Build.
